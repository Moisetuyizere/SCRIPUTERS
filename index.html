Sharing daily encouragement from the Word of God

"Trust in the LORD with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways submit to him, and he will make your paths straight."

— Proverbs 3:5-6

Reflection: When we surrender our plans to God, He directs our steps and guides our future with clarity and peace.

"The LORD is my strength and my shield; in him my heart trusts, and I am helped; my heart exults, and with my song I give thanks to him."

— Psalm 28:7

Reflection: Even during difficult seasons, God provides strength and protection when we rely completely on Him.

"Have I not commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged, for the LORD your God will be with you wherever you go."

— Joshua 1:9

Reflection: We never walk alone. Courage comes from knowing that God is with us step by step.

"For I know the plans I have for you, declares the LORD, plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future."

— Jeremiah 29:11

Reflection: God's vision for our lives is grounded in hope, goodness, and ultimate purpose, even when circumstances seem uncertain.

"I can do all this through him who gives me strength."

— Philippians 4:13

Reflection: Our capacity and perseverance do not originate from human effort alone, but through Christ who empowers us.

"And we know that in all things God works for the good of those who love him, who have been called according to his purpose."

— Romans 8:28

Reflection: Every challenge and blessing serves a higher purpose in God's master plan for our lives.
